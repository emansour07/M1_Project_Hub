How documents are matched

Names can include or omit revision and file extension, use upper/lower case, underscores or hyphens, and omit leading zeros in the stage or document serial. Drawing PDF and DWG representations share one identity. Confirmed old/new asset codes, ARX/SAP and the former FXX parcel are recognised. Stage, level and discipline remain distinct.

A changed serial number needs a saved match. Candidates with the same naming fields appear below for confirmation. Repeated MIDP numbers remain unresolved so separate deliverables are not combined.
