How to structure a useful requirement

Use one row for one information need. Identify the asset, discipline, system and element; explain how the information will be used; then specify the parameter, type, units, format and acceptance criteria. Add responsible teams and interface questions as they become known.

“Ready for coordination” and “Consolidated” are team working states. They do not approve engineering or issue anything to a subcontractor. No message is sent automatically.
